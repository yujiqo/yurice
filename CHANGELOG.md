## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

the first release. everything below is new.

### Added

- a niri desktop with a quickshell bar made of islands that grow into panels: workspaces, wifi and bluetooth, volume and brightness, battery with power profiles and caffeine, clock and calendar, media, weather, wallpapers, danbooru, light/dark theme, and your user profile.
- launcher with a built-in calculator (`alt+space`), clipboard history with images (`mod+y`), power menu (`mod+q`), screenshot tool (`print`) and a keybind cheatsheet that reads the niri config (`mod+shift+/`).
- two renamable modes that switch avatar, wallpaper and bar islands, and hide unsafe search filters.
- full keyboard navigation in every panel.
- material you colors from the wallpaper for the shell, niri, kitty, neovim, tmux and fastfetch, with light and dark on a schedule.
- wallpaper picker, wallhaven search and ai upscaling.
- lock screen with screen-off, lock and suspend timers, and a matching greeter on greetd.
- display settings with drag-to-arrange monitors and a confirm countdown.
- terminal setup: kitty, fish, starship, tmux, neovim, lazygit, less and fastfetch.
- `~/.config/yurice/custom/` overrides for niri, kitty, fish, tmux, neovim and the shell settings.
- installer for arch and debian 13 with uninstall, and nixos and home-manager modules.
- docs: a book on how every part is built, plus customizing, keybinds, troubleshooting, philosophy and contributing guides.
